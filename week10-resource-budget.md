### Risk-Based Cost Exposure
- Risk [Risk 1 – Clinic Testing Delays]: [if the six clinics take two extra weeks to finish testing,
- we will have to pay our team for two extra weeks of work.] — Potential Cost Exposure: $[$11,200 ]

  
- Risk [Risk 2 – System Setup Problems]: [if any clinic has internet or computer trouble during setup,
- we will need extra IT Support hours and outside tech help to fix it.] — Potential Cost Exposure: $[6,800]

**Recommended Contingency Reserve:** $[18,000]

[Instead of guessing a random percentage, I added up the cost of our two biggest risks ($11,200 + $6,800 = $18,000). 
This gives us a realistic safety net to cover extra pay or tech fixes if something goes wrong during the rollout.]
